# Personal website

The personal website presents Ethan Greentree's work and articles.

## Language

**Article**:
A published piece of writing by Ethan Greentree, identified by a title and publication date.
_Avoid_: Blog post, writing entry

**Project**:
Something Ethan Greentree made that stands on its own, shown with a status, description, and the tech used. An Article may be written about a Project and link to it.
_Avoid_: Portfolio item, case study
