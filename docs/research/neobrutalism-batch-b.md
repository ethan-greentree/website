# Neo-brutalism reference research: creative and brand sites

Researched 2026-10-11. Batch B evidence notes for the main design research. These ten candidates are **not ten verified current core neo-brutalist sites**. Several are adjacent graphic brutalism, one is mainly a historical reference, and some recognition remains unverified. A curated feature proves recognition, not popularity by traffic. No traffic ranking was researched.

Method: opened each first-party URL through web retrieval and consulted original creator case studies where available. No rendered browser inspection was performed by this researcher. Parent research may add screenshot observations separately. Typography, color, and interaction claims below are limited to identified source evidence; unknown fields are stated rather than invented. “Borrow” and “avoid” are design judgments for Ethan's earthy, chunky, cyberpunk/indie-web wishlist, including readable articles and sticky section labels.

## B1. Treize Grammes

**Live:** [13g.fr](https://www.13g.fr/). **Class:** graphic/interaction brutalism adjacent to neo; current appearance needs rendered confirmation. **Recognition:** [CSS Design Awards Website of the Day, 6 October 2024](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/treize-grammes/46361/). The live site presents branding, projects, agency material, and contact. Creator Thomas Carré's [first-person Codrops case study](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2024/10/10/case-study-treize-grammes-2024/) explains an activation concept: a fake button transforms into video and changes controls through a GSAP timeline. Exact current font and palette were not verified. **Borrow:** derive one interaction from the site's subject—opening a lab report could reveal evidence. **Avoid:** disguising navigation as non-obvious interactive art or requiring animation to reach projects.

## B2. FlowFest 2025

**Live:** [flowfest.co.uk](https://www.flowfest.co.uk/). **Class:** playful illustrated neo-adjacent; not a strict hard-shadow UI specimen. **Recognition:** [Awwwards listing](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/%2312181B/?page=7) records Honorable Mention and Developer Award; [GSAP's own feature](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/greensock_site-of-the-week-flowfest-2025-its-activity-7338566563463090176-P4O2) independently recognizes it. Live content has short section navigation, speaker portraits, activities, FAQ, and one repeated ticket action. [Developer's account](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dennissnellenberg_new-project-i-built-this-new-website-for-activity-7338534253900574721-oeHG) confirms playful scrolling and a sun reacting to the ticket button. Font/palette need visual verification. **Borrow:** intentional illustrated personality tied to content. **Avoid:** copying an event sales funnel or putting movement on every section.

## B3. Sui Overflow

**Live:** [overflow.sui.io](https://overflow.sui.io/), currently labeled 2026. **Class:** structural/technical neo candidate, current styling awaiting visual confirmation. **Recognition:** [Awwwards records the 2025 edition's Site of the Day and Developer Award](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/%236343E7/); do not award that honor to the current edition. Live navigation indexes overview, tracks, prizes, sponsors, and FAQ; numbered modules and literal date-like markup provide technical personality. Illustrations include blocks and stars. Exact font and current color tokens were not verified. **Borrow:** highly legible sectional navigation and real metadata. **Avoid:** ornamental loading progress, copied crypto messaging, or accidentally presenting an old event date as current. Ethan's lab index could use the structure without the industry branding.

## B4. Roze Bunker

**Live:** [rozebunker.nl](https://rozebunker.nl/). **Class:** expressive graphic/brand brutalism adjacent, current neo classification provisional. **Recognition:** [Awwwards listing](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/%23FFE632/?page=50) includes an Honorable Mention. Live text offers mission, flavors, business, contact, FAQ, a mission video, and a separate shop. It explicitly invites clicking falling bottles, grounding the playful imagery in its own product. Current homepage also advertises a successor shop; reference screenshots may predate that update. Typeface and colors were not independently verified. **Borrow:** meaningful real objects and a nature-related identity with personality. **Avoid:** dropping arbitrary icons around a generic hero, and making a moving object the only path to substantive information.

## B5. SUB:BIO STUDIOS

**Live:** [sub-bio-studios.com.au](https://sub-bio-studios.com.au/). **Class:** portfolio/graphic brutalism adjacent; exact current visual traits unverified. **Recognition:** discovered in a design examples roundup; no first-party award/recognition record established. It therefore does **not** meet a strong popularity claim. First-party text verifies a minimal Work/About/Contact menu and a work-focused sequence of client imagery, then direct email/social/location information. Font, palette, oversized type, and hidden hover-gallery behavior require rendered inspection rather than accepting third-party descriptions. **Borrow:** navigation with three understandable choices and visual evidence preceding extended self-description. **Avoid:** image-only project identification or hover-only discoveries; IT projects need explanatory titles and useful text alongside screenshots.

## B6. e—b agency

**Live:** [e-b.agency](https://e-b.agency/). **Class:** expressive typographic neo-adjacent candidate. **Recognition:** [Supahero's feature](https://supahero.io/hero/e-b-agency) identifies its hero; the agency's own broader award claims do not establish an award for this exact current homepage. Live material includes a strongly voiced introduction, project summaries, agency differentiators, news, and a detailed contact form. Source text supports personality and project sequencing; color, exact font, and motion remain unverified. **Borrow:** a distinctive human voice attached to specific work, and project descriptions that say what changed. **Avoid:** importing aggressive agency slogans, unverified rankings, or a large sales intake form into a personal IT portfolio.

## B7. Even/Odd

**Live:** [evenodd.studio](https://evenodd.studio/). **Class:** cinematic graphic brutalism adjacent; not established as core neo UI. **Recognition:** featured by a design roundup; no award for the site's current edition independently established. [Builder RNR's first-party case study](https://www.rnr.cool/work/even-odd) documents layered motion, transitions, scroll effects, and simultaneous video playback. Its project screenshots describe portraits and red/pink backgrounds. Live text separates a large Even/Odd identity, project titles, studio positioning, and contact. Exact typeface unverified; builder speed claims were not benchmarked. **Borrow:** confident identity and a clear work index. **Avoid:** multiple streaming videos on an IT blog or treating a builder's performance marketing as a measured guarantee.

## B8. Problem Studio

**Live URL resolves:** [problem.studio](https://www.problem.studio/), but yielded no parsed text. **Class:** historical typographic brutalism; current visuals unverified. **Recognition:** [One Page Love's 17 February 2020 feature](https://onepagelove.com/problem-studio) classifies Brutalism/Fun/Typographic and names Gosha Sans. It describes a full-width scrolling page, custom cursor, team credits, and sound effects. This is useful historical evidence, not proof the present site matches the captured design. Palette and current navigation cannot be asserted. **Borrow:** one deliberate typographic identity and a visibly human collaboration story. **Avoid:** compulsory sound, custom cursor dependence, or hiding contact behind a joke. Exclude from a “30 current verified sites” count until rendered inspection succeeds.

## B9. Twenty Nine / XXIX

**Live:** [xxix.co](https://www.xxix.co/). **Class:** historical neo reference; current classification unverified. A [2022 Digital Silk trend publication](https://www.digitalsilk.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Digital-Silk-2022-Web-Trends.pdf) used XXIX as an example, but it cannot establish today's styling or popularity. Current first-party text shows project/client/industry filters, services, research/writing, studio information, and a skip link. Exact typography and colors need visual inspection; do not reuse a 2022 screenshot as current evidence. **Borrow:** giving writing an equal place beside practical work and providing useful project metadata. **Avoid:** adopting a large agency's complex navigation before Ethan has enough material. Better as a caution about redesign drift than a core neo token reference.

## B10. Laser Me Out

**Live:** [lasermeout.com](https://www.lasermeout.com/). **Class:** explicit creator-described neo-brutalism; current continuity needs visual verification. **Recognition:** no independent award/gallery record established; retain as a first-party case-study reference, not popularity evidence. [Creator WAGEMUT](https://www.wagemut.studio/en/projects/lmo) documents Made Tommy type, red/yellow with warm accents, and 3D-to-line-art illustrations. It explains toning down an overly loud first draft and restructuring brochure-length copy. Current text has clear section links, comparisons, pricing, and FAQ. **Borrow:** one coherent illustration language and the discipline to reduce text density without abandoning character. **Avoid:** reproducing its long conversion-heavy homepage. This is the strongest source-grounded lesson in balancing loud design with readable structure.

## Implications and exclusions

- A heavy outline alone is not an identity. The useful common thread is a recognizable subject expressed through specific typography, objects, language, and page structure.
- Ethan's sticky labels can organize a real project/workshop index; an agency sales funnel does not need to be copied wholesale.
- Current browser screenshots should decide which candidates inform actual tokens. Several entries support structure/content lessons more strongly than palette/font claims.
- **Curry Cafe** (`curry.cafe`) and **OXYZ3** (`oxyz3.com`) failed retrieval; **Naked City Films** also failed in this pass. They were not substituted with unrelated businesses sharing names. Exclude from verified-live totals until checked another way.
- This batch establishes ten candidate analyses but only a smaller set with direct independent recognition. It must not be reported as ten proven popular current core neo-brutalist examples. If the final corpus needs thirty verified examples, replace weak entries or explicitly describe the corpus as core neo plus adjacent/historical comparisons.
