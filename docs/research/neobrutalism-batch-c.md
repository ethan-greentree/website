# Neobrutalism research: independent sites and portfolios

Research date: 2026-10-11. These are candidates for the main comparative study, not a traffic ranking. No reliable evidence establishes them as the ten most popular independent neobrutalist sites. The six community examples share a UI-kit lineage; treat that concentration as a limitation rather than six independent endorsements of the same component vocabulary.

This batch verified first-party pages through the web text reader. It did **not** inspect live rendered screenshots, mobile behavior, or motion. Consequently typography families, precise colors, borders, shadows, and interaction quality are deliberately unspecified here. Parent research can add visual findings separately after capture. Recommendations below are interpretations of the content organization, not claims about unobserved CSS.

## 1. Advibly

- First party: [advibly.com](https://advibly.com/).
- Recognition: [kit-maintainer showcase](https://neobrutalism.com/showcase), not popularity evidence.
- Classification: **historically/gallery-associated neo; current visual degree unverified**.
- Observed: the current page pairs a short opening proposition with a large output gallery, numbered workflow, interface demonstrations, and pricing. Product images dominate the extracted content.
- Borrow: actual artifacts can explain a project better than rows of skill icons. A project preview should show something Ethan made, with a short explanation.
- Avoid: translating a personal homepage into the site's long sales funnel, duplicated creative galleries, model lists, and pricing sections. Confirm the current design before copying the older gallery screenshot.

## Implications for Ethan's design system

1. Match components to content: a project preview needs an artifact and short account, a post listing needs title/date/context, and an article needs quiet reading space. Thick borders do not justify converting every item into an identical card.
2. Use a coherent motif. A restrained field journal or document/archive vocabulary would connect IT, dithered imagery, and personal writing; a whole desktop simulation would be a different and more demanding product decision.
3. Study generic kit sites as negative controls too. Repeated loud badges, benefit grids, sales CTAs, pricing blocks, and stock graphics explain how neo-brutalism itself can become generic.
4. Require actual screenshot evidence before specifying type/color/composition from these candidates. This batch's strongest immediate findings concern information architecture and reference reliability.
5. Preserve truth in content. Neither these portfolios' achievements nor their confident slogans should become invented claims about Ethan.
