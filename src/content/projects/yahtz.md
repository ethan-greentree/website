Yahtz is a Yahtzee-inspired game I made for my wife, who has a soft spot for the original. Instead of rolling down a scorecard, you place dice on a 5 × 5 board, so earlier turns shape the hands you can make later.

Jokers chosen at the start of a game change how you play, such as scoring the diagonal or rewarding certain dice. Events add scoring goals, some lasting the whole game and some changing each round.

It's built with the Usagi Engine in Lua, so it runs on both Windows and Mac, and here in the browser. I used it to practise guiding AI-assisted development through questions, design docs, and iteration.
