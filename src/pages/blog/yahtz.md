Yahtz is a Yahtzee inspired game made with the [Usagi Engine](https://usagiengine.com/). It was made for my wife, who has a soft spot for the game!

## Why Usagi?

I chose Usagi as it was a small engine that has cross platform support out of the box! This was important as my wife uses a Mac computer while I am on Windows.

## Playing Yahtz

Yahtz uses a 5 x 5 grid, where you can place dice to try and make the best Yahtzee hands you can to try and beat your high score. To assist you in making high scores, there are two ways to make extra points. Jokers and Events.

Jokers are cards that are chosen at the beginning of the game and impact how you play. For example, 'Crossroads' allows you to score the diagonal region of the board, offering more ways to score. Others recommend certain types of dice like evens or odds.

Events are different, there is one harder event that takes place over the whole game, and smaller events that change each round, these events offer scoring options during play!

## Is this balanced?

Absolutely not, this game has many flaws balance being foremost of them. But we find it fun and enjoyable to play!

## How was it made?

This game was made as an exercise in AI assisted development, meaning that it was made with the assistance of Codex. The decision making was still done by myself through an interview style session where I would answer a bunch of questions about how I wanted the game to run and feel.
